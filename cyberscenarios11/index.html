Identity & Access Crisis

Welcome to ConnectSphere, Cyber Security Engineer! After integrating the customer portal with SSO (Single Sign-On), the alarms started ringing. The help desk is flooded with complaints about users having unexpected privileges and some accounts being taken over. Your mission: find the root cause of this complex identity crisis, close the security holes, and harden the system architecture.

Simulation Roles

  • Your Role: Cyber Security Engineer You will deeply inspect the SSO/OIDC configuration, fix the vulnerabilities, and mitigate future risks.

  • Compliance Department Concerned about data privacy regulations and potential legal consequences.

APT Crisis Simulation: The CORESEC Incident

Welcome, new CISO of CORESEC Technologies! Your company, a provider of critical infrastructure monitoring services to the financial sector, has been targeted by a state-sponsored APT (Advanced Persistent Threat) group in a simultaneous, multi-country attack. You are facing DNS manipulation, physical breaches, data encryption, and deepfake disinformation campaigns. Your mission: Manage the technical chaos, withstand geopolitical pressure, and lead the company out of this complex crisis!

Simulation Roles

  • Your Role: CISO (Chief Information Security Officer) You are responsible for managing the crisis in all its aspects and making strategic decisions.
  • CEO Focused on the company's overall reputation, stock value, and business continuity.
  • CTO (Chief Technology Officer) Responsible for technical recovery operations and system reconstruction.
  • Legal Counsel Handles legal obligations, regulations (GDPR), and potential lawsuits.
  • Communications Director Manages media, press, public opinion, and internal communications.
  • Government Affairs Officer Communicates with national agencies and geopolitical stakeholders.

Key Metrics

  • Operational Continuity The uptime rate of critical financial services. (Start: 100%)
  • Financial Loss The cost of the crisis to the company (recovery, fines, losses). (Start: $0)
  • Customer & Stakeholder Trust The confidence of customers and shareholders in the company. (Start: 100)
  • International Reputation Your company's image in the public eye and on the international stage. (Start: 100)

Breach of Trust Simulation

           

Welcome, Incident Responder! Today you will face a crisis that goes beyond technology: **The Insider Threat**. Leaked data, a falling reputation, and shattered trust... In this simulation, you will not only solve technical problems but also make tough decisions involving human psychology, corporate culture, and ethical dilemmas. Every choice you make will determine the course of the crisis and the fate of the company. Are you ready?

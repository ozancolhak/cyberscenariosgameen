VPN & Internal Network Penetration Test Simulation

Welcome. In this new penetration testing project, your target is a company's VPN network. You will infiltrate from the outside via VPN access and look for privilege escalation opportunities by gathering information from within. Your task is to assess the potential for infiltration into the corporate network and to prove the vulnerabilities within ethical boundaries. Your decisions will directly impact both the success of the project and the security of the company network!

Your Simulation Role

  • Your Role: Penetration Tester You are responsible for finding vulnerabilities in the given network and proving them within ethical limits.
  • Target: Corporate VPN & Internal Network Gaining access to the company's VPN portal and discovering resources on the internal network.

  • Network Performance Damage to the application's operation. (Initial: 100%)

Phase 1: OSINT & Naming Schema

Target: Extracting VPN Usernames

In the first phase of your penetration test, you are gathering information about the company from open sources (OSINT). Your goal is to find email and naming schemas to guess valid usernames that can be used on the VPN portal.

Which OSINT technique will you apply to extract usernames?

Phase 2: Password Spray Plan (Lockout-Safe)

Target: Gaining VPN Access

You will now try to gain access to the VPN portal using the usernames obtained in the previous phase. You need to try a list of common passwords without violating the company's account lockout policies and without making noise.

Which password attack plan will you implement without triggering account lockouts?

Phase 3: Bypassing MFA / Portal Perimeters

Target: Accessing the VPN

Your Password Spray attack was successful, and you have compromised a user's credentials. However, when you try to access the VPN portal, you are blocked by MFA (Multi-Factor Authentication). Your goal at this stage is to bypass this barrier by finding a weak MFA configuration or an old access protocol.

What method will you try when faced with an MFA barrier?

Phase 4: Low-Noise Internal Reconnaissance

Target: Discovering Internal Network Resources

You have gained access to the internal network via VPN. Your objective now is to gather information about internal resources, servers, and potential sources of information leakage with as little noise as possible. This will lay the groundwork for privilege escalation in the next phase.

Which reconnaissance methods will you use to explore the internal network without making noise?

Phase 5: Credential Hunting (Config/Script)

Target: Finding Credentials for Privilege Escalation

During your internal network reconnaissance, you found a `deploy.ps1` or `db.yml` file on a file share that potentially contains sensitive information. Your goal is to find critical credentials embedded in these files, such as usernames, passwords, or connection strings, and use them to elevate your privileges.

How will you extract credentials from sensitive files?

Phase 6: Proof of Impact with a Service Account

Target: Creating a Proof of Concept

In the previous phase, you compromised the credentials of a service account. This account has critical privileges on the network. Your goal is to use these privileges to create a Proof of Concept without causing permanent damage to the system.

How will you create proof of your privilege escalation?

Phase 7: Report & Hardening

Target: Comprehensive Reporting and Improvement

You have successfully completed the penetration test and demonstrated the entire vulnerability chain, from the VPN to the internal network. Now, you need to prepare a report to present your findings to management and the technical team and make recommendations for permanent improvements.

What reporting and hardening strategy will you follow to complete your project?

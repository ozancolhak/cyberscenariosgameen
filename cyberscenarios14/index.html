Virtualization Network Issue Simulation

Welcome! Your company's critical e-commerce platform, hosted on your virtualization infrastructure, is experiencing severe network issues. Customer complaints are increasing, and latency and packet loss are at abnormal levels. As a senior Network and Virtualization Engineer, you are tasked with solving this complex problem and ensuring system stability.

Your Simulation Role

  • Your Role: Senior Network and Virtualization Engineer As the senior engineer responsible for the network performance of the virtualization infrastructure, you must find the source of the problem and solve it permanently.
  • Virtualization Platform Your entire infrastructure, including VMware ESXi, vCenter, virtual switches (vSwitch), and port groups.
  • Physical Network Infrastructure The physical switches, cabling, and routers to which the servers are connected.

Key Metrics

  • Quality of Service (QoS) The rate of latency and packet loss affecting customer experience. (Initial: Critical)
  • Resource Utilization The effective utilization rate of resources like network, CPU, RAM on the physical host. (Initial: Abnormal)
  • Customer Reputation Damage to the company's credibility. (Initial: Low)
  • Cost The financial equivalent of service outages and potential customer loss. (Initial: Increasing)
